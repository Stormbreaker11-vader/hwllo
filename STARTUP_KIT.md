## 💼 Business Value Proposition

### Problem We Solve
- **Context Loss**: Users lose all conversation history when switching AI models or hitting token limits
- **Redundant Explanations**: Teams waste 80% of time re-explaining context across sessions
- **Platform Lock-in**: No way to port knowledge between ChatGPT, Claude, local LLMs, etc.

### Our Solution
✓ **Portable Context**: Copy-paste entire knowledge graphs anywhere  
✓ **Platform Agnostic**: Works with ANY AI model, browser, or machine  
✓ **Zero Setup**: No accounts, no cloud, no dependencies  
✓ **Enterprise Ready**: Local-first, encrypted exports, audit trails
